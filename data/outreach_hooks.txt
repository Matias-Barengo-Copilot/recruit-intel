P1 DENTAL PARTNERS - RECRUITMENT OUTREACH HOOKS

=== ALUMNI TIES ===
- "I noticed you're a {SCHOOL} alum — we have an opportunity in {TARGET_CITY}"
- "As a fellow {SCHOOL} graduate, I wanted to reach out about..."
- "We have a strong {SCHOOL} alumni network here in {TARGET_CITY}"
- "{TARGET_CITY} is home to your alma mater, {SCHOOL}"

=== TAX SAVINGS ===
- "{STATE} tax rates would save you approximately ${TAX_SAVINGS}/year vs. {CURRENT_STATE}"
- "Moving to {STATE} could mean ${TAX_SAVINGS} in annual tax savings"
- "Lower cost of living and ${TAX_SAVINGS} in tax benefits compared to {CURRENT_STATE}"
- "{STATE} offers significant tax advantages for high earners like yourself"

=== FAMILY/SPOUSE TIES ===
- "I saw your spouse is from {SPOUSE_LOCATION} — closer to family might appeal"
- "Your LinkedIn mentions {FAMILY_LOCATION} roots — returning to the Midwest?"
- "With family ties to {REGION}, this could be a homecoming opportunity"
- "Your spouse's background in {SPOUSE_FIELD} aligns well with {TARGET_CITY}'s growing {INDUSTRY}"

=== GEOGRAPHIC PROXIMITY ===
- "You're already in {CURRENT_CITY}, just {DISTANCE} from {TARGET_CITY}"
- "Short relocation from {CURRENT_CITY} — same region, better opportunity"
- "Midwest location keeps you close to {CURRENT_STATE} connections"

=== CAREER GROWTH ===
- "Lead endodontist role at a 60+ location network"
- "Partnership track within 3-5 years"
- "Clinical autonomy with enterprise support infrastructure"
- "Practice ownership opportunities as we expand regionally"

=== LIFESTYLE ===
- "Lower cost of living than {CURRENT_CITY} — same income goes further"
- "{TARGET_CITY} offers {LIFESTYLE_FEATURE} that you mentioned enjoying"
- "Family-friendly community with excellent schools"
- "Vibrant downtown, growing tech scene, affordable housing"

=== SAMPLE COMBINATION HOOKS ===
Hook 1: Alumni + Tax + Family
"Hi Dr. {LAST_NAME}, I noticed you're a Notre Dame alum — we have an endodontics opportunity in South Bend (your alma mater town!). Indiana's tax rates would save you ~$18K/year vs. Illinois. Your spouse's LinkedIn shows ties to the Midwest — would you be open to a conversation about returning to the region?"

Hook 2: Location + Career + Lifestyle
"Dr. {LAST_NAME}, you're currently in {CURRENT_CITY}, just 90 minutes from our South Bend location. We're looking for a lead endodontist with partnership potential. Lower cost of living, excellent schools, and a growing practice network. Worth a conversation?"

Hook 3: School + Family + Tax
"Dr. {LAST_NAME}, as a {SCHOOL} graduate with family in {FAMILY_LOCATION}, this opportunity might interest you. Lead endo role in {TARGET_CITY}, ${TAX_SAVINGS} in annual tax savings vs. {CURRENT_STATE}, and partnership track. Can we schedule a call?"
